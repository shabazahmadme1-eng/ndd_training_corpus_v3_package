## Assay 1: proposed task `binding` (REVIEW REQUIRED)
- URN: urn:mavedb:00000048-b-1 | license: CC0 | published: 2020-11-11
- Title: CXCR4 CXCL12 binding
- Experiment: CXCR4 CXCL12 binding (urn:mavedb:00000048-b)
- Variants: 7021 listed, 6645 mapped missense, 0 WT mismatches, offset 0, target len 1053
- Method: The human CXCR4 sequence was generated by oligo assembly and fused to a c-myc tag. SSM libraries were generated by overlapping PCR using primers with an NNK codon, as described by Procko et al, 2013.
The SSM library was transfected into CXCR4-knockout human Expi293F cells using pCEP4. 
Surface receptor expression was measured by anti-myc-Alexa staining and cells were sorted on a BD FACSAria II.
Cells were incubated with CXCL12 fused to superfolder GFP (sf-GFP) and sorted for interaction with CXCL12-sfGFP. 
Total RNA was extracted and PCR-amplified before sequencing by Illumina MiSeq v 3 or HiSeq 2500. 

Raw data available from GEO under accession [GSE100368](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE100368). This experiment utilised site-saturation mutagenesis (SSM) to measure the functional consequences of mutations in the human chemokine receptor, CXCR4 and to map ligand interaction sites. Cells were selected for binding to the CXCR4 physiological ligand CXCL12.
