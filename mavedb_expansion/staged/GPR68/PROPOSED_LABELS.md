Our DIMPLE platform was used to generate the GPR68 deep mutational library51. Briefly, we designed the library to contain all missense mutations at each position in GPR68. This DNA was resuspended and the sublibrary fragments were amplified using PrimeStar GXL DNA polymerase and fragment-specific primers (Table S7).
